# Hair Fall in Bhubaneswar: Is It the Water, Stress, or Something Else?

Have you noticed more hair falling out while washing or combing? Maybe your hair doesn't feel as thick as it used to, or you've started noticing a wider parting, a receding hairline, or thinning around your temples.

For some people, these changes happen gradually. For others, they seem to begin after moving to a new city, changing their routine, or going through a stressful period.

If you're living in Bhubaneswar, you may have wondered whether the local water, weather, diet, or lifestyle is affecting your hair.

It's a reasonable question. But before blaming any one factor, it's worth understanding what might actually be happening.

Hair shedding, thinning, breakage, and scalp problems can look similar at first. Their causes, however, can be quite different.

## Why Does Hair Start Falling More Than Usual?

Hair naturally goes through a cycle of growth, rest, and shedding. Losing some hair every day is normal.

The concern usually begins when the amount increases noticeably, the hair feels less dense, or the scalp becomes more visible.

Several things can contribute to these changes. Illness, major stress, rapid weight loss, nutritional problems, and certain medical conditions may lead to excessive shedding. Hereditary factors can also cause gradual hair thinning.

One condition known as *telogen effluvium* can cause increased shedding after a significant physical or emotional trigger.

Interestingly, the shedding doesn't always begin immediately.

A 2019 expert consensus published in the *International Journal of Trichology* explains that telogen effluvium may become noticeable around two to three months after a triggering event.

This means someone who experienced an illness, a difficult examination period, or another major change may notice increased hair shedding weeks later.

However, not every case of hair fall is stress-related. Persistent shedding deserves attention, especially when its cause isn't clear.

## Could Bhubaneswar's Water Be Affecting Your Hair?

Water quality is one of the first things people question when their hair starts feeling different after moving to a new place.

A student shifting into a hostel in Patia, for example, may notice that their hair feels rougher than it did at home. Someone moving into a rented apartment might find that their hair tangles more easily or feels dry even after washing.

It's natural to suspect the bathing water, particularly when the change follows a move.

And water quality really can differ from one area to another.

A study by Samal and Tarai, published in *F1000Research*, examined piped-water quality across 67 wards of Bhubaneswar.

The researchers found differences in water hardness between the northern, south-western, and south-eastern parts of the city. Average hardness ranged from approximately 117 to 186 mg/L across the three zones.

The study generally classified the water quality in the surveyed zones as good or excellent under its water-quality index, although individual parameters varied.

These findings tell us that water characteristics are not identical throughout Bhubaneswar. They do not, however, show that local water causes hair loss.

### Does hard water damage hair?

Hard water contains dissolved minerals, mainly calcium and magnesium. These minerals may influence how hair behaves during washing, although the scientific findings are not entirely consistent.

A laboratory study published in the *International Journal of Trichology* in 2018 found that hair samples exposed to hard water had lower tensile strength under the conditions tested.

An earlier study in the same journal, published in 2013, did not find a statistically significant difference in hair strength or elasticity between hard-water-treated and distilled-water-treated samples.

In other words, some experimental findings suggest that hard water may affect the hair shaft, but there is not enough evidence to conclude that it causes permanent baldness.

This distinction is important.

Hair becoming rough, dry, or prone to breakage is not the same as hair follicles gradually producing thinner hair.

If your hairline is receding or your crown is becoming visibly thinner, changing the water alone may not address the underlying cause.

## Why Are Some People Losing Hair in Their Early Twenties?

Noticing a receding hairline at 20 or 22 can be worrying, especially when friends of the same age seem to have thick hair.

But hair loss doesn't always begin in middle age.

Androgenetic alopecia, commonly called pattern hair loss, can begin in early adulthood. Genetics and sensitivity to certain hormones play important roles.

In people with this condition, affected hair follicles gradually produce finer and shorter hairs. Over time, this can make the hairline appear higher or the crown less dense.

The pattern can differ between men and women. Some people notice thinning at the temples, while others first observe reduced density or a widening central part.

Research published in the *Journal of Clinical Medicine* in 2024 describes how trichoscopy, a close examination of the scalp and hair, can help identify features associated with androgenetic alopecia.

This is one reason why understanding the pattern of hair loss matters. Someone experiencing temporary shedding may need a very different approach from someone with progressive hereditary thinning.

## What About Dandruff, Itching, Dry Scalp, and Rough Hair?

Hair concerns aren't always about how much hair is falling.

Sometimes the problem is an itchy scalp that doesn't seem to settle. Sometimes it's recurring dandruff, greasy flakes, dryness, or hair that becomes difficult to manage.

These problems can be uncomfortable, and trying one shampoo after another may not always help.

Dandruff can be associated with seborrheic dermatitis, a condition that causes scalp flaking and inflammation. Other scalp conditions can produce similar symptoms, so appearance alone may not reveal the cause.

The American Academy of Dermatology explains that seborrheic dermatitis may be managed with suitable medicated shampoos and other treatments, depending on the condition and its severity.

Dry or rough hair, meanwhile, can have different explanations. Frequent heat styling, chemical treatments, rough handling, and environmental factors can all affect the hair shaft.

A flaky scalp, brittle hair, and a receding hairline are not necessarily parts of the same problem.

## Is Changing Shampoo, Oil, or Taking Supplements Enough?

When hair problems begin, changing products often feels like the easiest thing to try.

Someone recommends an oil. Another person suggests a serum. Social media may recommend vitamins, a shower filter, or a particular shampoo.

Some of these products may be useful for specific concerns. A conditioner, for example, can help improve how dry or damaged hair feels. A suitable dandruff shampoo may help manage certain scalp conditions.

But there is no single product that treats every cause of hair loss.

If someone has pattern hair loss, simply applying more oil will not reliably reverse follicle miniaturization. Similarly, vitamin supplements are not automatically helpful when a nutritional deficiency has not been identified.

The more useful question is not necessarily which product is popular.

It's whether that product addresses the actual problem.

## Can Hair Grow Back Once It Starts Thinning?

This is often the question people care about most.

The answer depends on why the hair is being lost.

In some cases, excessive shedding improves as the underlying trigger resolves. Hair density may gradually recover, although the process can take time.

For certain forms of pattern hair loss, medical treatments can sometimes slow progression or improve density. Some people require ongoing treatment to maintain the benefits.

Other forms of hair loss may be difficult to reverse, particularly when the follicles have been permanently damaged.

Even with appropriate treatment, results differ from person to person.

Hair grows slowly, and meaningful changes may take several months to become visible.

This is why expectations should be based on the diagnosed condition rather than advertisements promising rapid or complete regrowth.

## When Should Hair Fall Be Checked?

A few extra strands during washing do not necessarily mean something is wrong.

However, persistent excessive shedding, a gradually widening part, a receding hairline, or noticeable loss of density can be reasons to seek medical advice.

Sudden bald patches, scalp pain, significant redness, or signs of scarring also deserve timely assessment.

A qualified medical professional may consider the pattern and duration of hair loss, relevant family history, recent health changes, scalp symptoms, and previous treatments.

Sometimes a clinical examination provides much of the information needed. In other situations, further investigation may be appropriate.

Not everyone with hair fall needs the same tests, medicines, or procedures.

Understanding the likely cause helps determine which options are reasonable.

## A Few Common Questions About Hair Fall in Bhubaneswar

### Can hard water make my hair rough?

Water hardness may influence hair-shaft properties and how hair feels after washing. However, research findings are mixed, and dryness or roughness can have several other causes.

### Why did my hair start shedding after moving to Bhubaneswar?

The timing may lead you to suspect the water or climate, but relocation can also coincide with changes in diet, stress, illness, sleep, and hair-care habits. The cause cannot be determined from the timing alone.

### Is a receding hairline at 20 always a sign of baldness?

Not always. Hairlines naturally vary, but progressive recession or thinning can sometimes indicate pattern hair loss. An assessment can help distinguish normal variation from a developing condition.

### Does dandruff lead to permanent hair loss?

Ordinary dandruff is not generally considered a direct cause of permanent baldness. However, significant scalp inflammation and certain conditions that resemble dandruff may be associated with hair problems.

### Will hair grow back if the shedding stops?

It may, depending on the cause. Some types of shedding are reversible, while other forms of hair loss require ongoing management or may not fully recover.

## Understanding Your Hair Is More Useful Than Guessing

Hair fall can be frustrating, particularly when it begins unexpectedly or continues despite trying different products.

For someone living in Bhubaneswar, questions about bathing water, weather, nutrition, stress, and hereditary hair loss are understandable.

But there is no single explanation that fits everyone.

Dry hair may be related to damage along the hair shaft. Increased shedding may follow an illness or another physical trigger. A gradually receding hairline may have a hereditary component. Persistent itching or dandruff may point towards a scalp condition.

These differences matter.

Research can help us understand how hair behaves and what may contribute to common problems. It also reminds us that an explanation that sounds convincing is not necessarily the correct one for every individual.

Rather than assuming that every hair concern needs a new product or an expensive treatment, it makes sense to first understand what has changed and what might be causing it.

**When the cause is clearer, decisions about hair care and treatment can be better informed.**
